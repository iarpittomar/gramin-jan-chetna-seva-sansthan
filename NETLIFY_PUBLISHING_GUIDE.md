   👉 **[app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop)**

2. *(Recommended)* Sign up for a **Free Netlify Account** using your Email or Google account (`grameenjanchetnasewasansthan@gmail.com`). This ensures your website stays permanently under your account.

3. Open **Finder** on your Mac (or File Explorer).

4. Navigate to your project folder location:

5. **Drag and drop the entire `gramin-jan-chetna-seva-sansthan` folder** directly onto the Netlify webpage upload box.

6. 🎉 **Congratulations!** Your website is now published live on the internet with a free SSL Security Certificate (🔒 HTTPS).

## 🏷️ STEP 2: Set Up Your Free Custom Domain Name

When you first upload, Netlify creates a random web link (e.g., `elastic-bhabha-123456.netlify.app`).  
Follow these steps to change it to your official free NGO address:

1. On your Netlify dashboard, click **Site Configuration** (or **Site Settings**).
2. Click **Change site name**.
3. Type your preferred name, for example:  
   - `graminjanchetna`  
   - `gramin-jan-chetna-basti`  
   - `graminjanchetnasewasansthan`
4. Click **Save**.

Your official live website address will now be:  
👉 **`https://graminjanchetna.netlify.app`**

*(This domain name is 100% free forever, includes SSL security encryption 🔒, and has unlimited visitor bandwidth).*

## 🌐 STEP 3: (Optional) Adding a Custom `.org` Domain Name Later

If your NGO eventually decides to purchase a custom domain like `graminjanchetna.org` or `graminjanchetna.org.in` (costs ~$8 to $12 per year from Namecheap, GoDaddy, or Hostinger):

1. In your Netlify site dashboard, go to **Domain Management > Add custom domain**.
2. Enter your domain name (e.g., `graminjanchetna.org`).
3. Netlify will show you 4 DNS Name Servers (e.g., `dns1.p01.nsone.net`).
4. Copy those 4 lines into your domain provider's DNS settings (GoDaddy/Hostinger).
5. Netlify will automatically secure your domain with free HTTPS within 15 minutes!

Whenever you add new photos or edit text:

2. Click on your site > **Deploys**.
3. Drag and drop your updated `gramin-jan-chetna-seva-sansthan` folder into the drop zone.
4. Your live website will update automatically in 5 seconds!
